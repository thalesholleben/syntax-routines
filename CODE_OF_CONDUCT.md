# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: treat everyone with respect, criticize ideas rather than people, and do not use the
project's spaces for harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.

Reports of unacceptable behavior: contato@syntaxlab.com.br. The maintainers reply within 5
business days and may remove comments, commits, issues and contributions that violate this code.
